How to try it

Look ahead.
Then blink.

Use good lighting and keep your face in view. A normal blink should pass quickly. Closing both eyes for 1.5 seconds triggers the prolonged-closure state.

Eye shape, glasses, lighting, and head angle affect the estimate. The fixed eye-ratio threshold is a demonstration heuristic, not a sleep diagnosis.

Use while stationary. This educational demo is not a driving safety system.

Camera help & technical details

Allow camera access when your browser asks. If it is blocked, use your browser's camera settings and try again. A camera-free sample is always available.

The camera model and vision engine download from Google and jsDelivr when needed. Your camera frames are processed locally in a worker; the sample uses synthetic measurements without downloading a model.
